📋 System Guidelines

This system predicts student performance using machine learning based on study hours, previous scores, extracurricular activities, sleep hours, and practice papers.

All inputs are validated through a 4-layer system to ensure realistic data before making predictions.

The system will classify your input as Human (valid), Invalid (format errors), Unrealistic (violates constraints), or Suspicious (unusual patterns).

Enter values within realistic ranges: study hours (0-24), previous scores (0-100), sleep hours (0-24), and sample papers (0-50).

Note: Hours studied + sleep hours cannot exceed 24 hours per day.
